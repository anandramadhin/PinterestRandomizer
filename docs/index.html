Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 22, 2026

Pinterest Randomizer is a personal project that helps users randomly select images from their own Pinterest boards.

Information We Access

When you authorize Pinterest Randomizer, the application may access your Pinterest boards and pins solely to provide the application's functionality.

How Your Information Is Used

  • Display your Pinterest boards.
  • Retrieve pins from your selected boards.
  • Randomly display images for inspiration.

Data Storage

At this time, Pinterest Randomizer does not permanently store your Pinterest content on external servers. Temporary application data may be stored locally within your browser to improve your experience.

Sharing of Information

Your Pinterest information is not sold or shared with third parties.
